# 🏦 Project Amani
### Core Banking Transformation & Data Migration

---

> **Independent portfolio case study developed for professional demonstration purposes.**
>
> <b>Zawadi Bank Plc</b>, a fictional mid-tier commercial bank serving approximately **2.4 million customers**, undertook a large-scale core banking modernization programme to migrate from a 16-year-old legacy banking platform to **Temenos Transact**.
>
> As the illustrative **Data Migration Project Manager**, I led the planning, governance, data quality remediation, reconciliation strategy, and cutover readiness activities for a **USD 2.1 million workstream** within an **USD 18.4 million transformation programme**.

The migration workstream was responsible for ensuring the accurate transfer of customer, account, product, and transaction data from the legacy environment into Temenos Transact while maintaining regulatory compliance, operational continuity, and financial integrity.

Success depended on reducing data quality defects, executing multiple mock migrations, and achieving near-perfect reconciliation outcomes prior to production cutover.

---

# 🚨 Business Challenge

The bank's legacy platform had become a major operational and strategic constraint.

### Key Pain Points

🔴 Unsupported proprietary architecture

🔴 Annual maintenance costs approaching **USD 640,000**

🔴 Manual reconciliation consuming approximately **420 staff-hours per month**

🔴 Inability to meet emerging real-time regulatory reporting requirements

🔴 Duplicate customer records and incomplete KYC information

🔴 Slower product development compared to competitors

Without intervention, these limitations would continue increasing operational risk and hinder the bank's digital transformation ambitions.

---

# 🏗 Programme Overview

Project Amani was established as an enterprise-wide transformation initiative consisting of six interconnected workstreams:

| Workstream | Focus Area |
|------------|------------|
| Data Migration | Customer, account and transaction migration |
| Core Platform Configuration | Temenos setup and customization |
| Channels & Integration | System interfaces and APIs |
| Testing & QA | Validation and acceptance testing |
| Change & Training | User readiness and adoption |
| Regulatory & Compliance | Compliance and governance assurance |

The Data Migration Workstream was considered the highest-risk workstream because it involved movement of sensitive customer and financial data.

# 🔄 Migration Lifecycle

```text
Discovery
   ↓
Profiling
   ↓
Data Mapping
   ↓
Data Cleansing
   ↓
Transformation
   ↓
Validation
   ↓
Mock Run 1
   ↓
Mock Run 2
   ↓
Mock Run 3
   ↓
Production Cutover
   ↓
Hypercare
   ↓
Post-Migration Review
```

The approach emphasized early defect detection through multiple migration rehearsals and progressive reconciliation targets.

# 📅 Delivery Roadmap

| Phase | Duration | Milestone |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| Discovery & Profiling | Months 1-2 | Data Baseline Established |
| Mapping & Cleansing | Months 2-4 | Mapping Sign-off |
| Transformation Build | Months 4-6 | ETL Development Completed |
| Mock Run 1 | Month 6 | Initial Defect Discovery |

# ⚠ Risk Assessment

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Poor Data Quality | High | Early profiling and cleansing |
| Reconciliation Gaps | High | Three staged mock migrations |
| Cutover Downtime | Medium | Rehearsed runbook and rollback strategy |
| Specialist Attrition | Medium | Cross-training and documentation |
