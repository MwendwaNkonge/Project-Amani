# 🎯 Power–Interest Grid

## Overview

The Power–Interest Grid is used to identify stakeholders and determine the most effective engagement strategy throughout the Project Amani migration lifecycle.

---

## Stakeholder Mapping

```text
                HIGH POWER
                     ↑

  Keep Satisfied     │     Manage Closely
                     │
  • Regulators       │     • Executive Sponsor
  • Compliance Team  │     • Project Manager
                     │
─────────────────────┼─────────────────────→ HIGH INTEREST

       Monitor       │      Keep Informed
                     │
  • External Vendors │     • End Users
                     │     • Support Teams
                     │
                LOW POWER
```

---

## Stakeholder Matrix

| Stakeholder | Power | Interest | Engagement Strategy |
|------------|---------|----------|---------------------|
| Executive Sponsor | High | High | Manage Closely |
| Project Manager | High | High | Manage Closely |
| IT Team | Medium | High | Keep Informed |
| End Users | Low | High | Keep Informed |
| Vendors | Medium | Low | Monitor |
| Regulators | High | Low | Keep Satisfied |

---

## Communication Plan

### 🔴 Manage Closely
- Weekly steering committee meetings
- Decision-making workshops
- Progress reporting

### 🟡 Keep Satisfied
- Monthly executive updates
- Compliance reviews
- Governance reporting

### 🔵 Keep Informed
- Training sessions
- Project newsletters
- Change management communications

### ⚪ Monitor
- Periodic status checks
- Contract performance reviews

---

## Key Takeaways

✅ High-power, high-interest stakeholders require continuous engagement.

✅ High-power, low-interest stakeholders should be kept satisfied without overwhelming them.

✅ High-interest, low-power stakeholders require regular communication and support.

✅ Low-power, low-interest stakeholders require minimal monitoring.
