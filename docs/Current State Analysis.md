## 🌍 Executive Summary

Project Amani seeks to transform the existing operational environment through a structured migration approach. Before defining the future state, a comprehensive assessment was conducted to understand the current processes, systems, stakeholder landscape, performance constraints, and operational challenges.

The assessment revealed opportunities to improve efficiency, reduce risk exposure, strengthen governance, and enhance stakeholder engagement throughout the migration journey.
